I am a second-year PhD candidate at the Sorbonne School of Economics at University of Paris 1 Panthéon-Sorbonne under the supervision of both Prof. Lisa Chauvet and Prof. Julieta Peveri.

My research interests lie within the political economy literature, particularly, in the use of politics as an informal insurance mechanism in developing countries where state-provided safety nets are not so easily available. My research so far has mainly focused on the interaction between clientelism and political systems from the clients' perspective. Additionally, I do research related to informal settlements and their political behavior, as well as their strategies for risk prevention. 

I hold a Master’s degree in Economics with a emphasis on Development Economics from University of Paris 1 Panthéon-Sorbonne and both a Bachelor’s degree in Economics and in Political Sciencie  from Pontificia Universidad Javeriana Cali, Colombia.
